🎯 How to Play Carrom Pool

Master the board with these rules and strategies

📋 Game Objective

Pocket all your colored disks (White) or (Black) before your opponent does. The player who pockets all their disks AND covers the Queen wins the game!

🎮 Controls

🖱️

Aim

Drag from striker to aim direction

⚡

Power

Drag further for more power

🎯

Strike

Release to shoot or press SPACE

⏱️

Timer

You have 30 seconds per turn

👑 The Queen (Red Disk)

The Queen is the most important disk:

  • Pocket the Queen to gain 3 bonus points
  • After pocketing the Queen, you MUST pocket one of your own disks in the same or next turn to "cover" it
  • If you fail to cover the Queen, it returns to the center of the board
  • You cannot win the game without covering the Queen (unless opponent has no disks left)

⚠️ Fouls (Penalties)

Committing a foul results in:

  • Striker Pocketed - Your turn ends and one of your pocketed disks returns to the board
  • Opponent's Disk Pocketed - Your turn ends and the disk is returned to the board
  • Queen Pocketed Without Cover - Queen returns to center
  • No Disk Hit - Your turn ends (striker must hit at least one disk)

🎯 Scoring System

Action Points Effect
Pocket your disk +1 Continue turn
Pocket Queen +3 Must cover in next shot
Cover Queen Queen secured Can win game now
Foul committed -1 (disk returned) Turn ends

🏆 Winning Conditions

You win the game when:

  • All your colored disks are pocketed
  • The Queen has been covered
  • You have more points than your opponent

💡 Strategy Tips

Aim for the Queen early when you have a clear shot - the 3 bonus points can be a game-changer!
Use the walls to bounce your striker and hit disks in difficult positions.
Don't use maximum power - controlled shots are more accurate and less likely to result in fouls.
If you can't pocket your own disk, try to break up clusters to set up your next turn.
Watch the timer! You have 30 seconds per turn - plan your shot quickly.
